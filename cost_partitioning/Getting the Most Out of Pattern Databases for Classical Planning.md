# Summary
You can combine pattern databases in interesting ways to get really strong heuristics.  Specifically, you can solve an LP to figure out how to combine the heuristics effectively.

## Contributions
* Show exactly how much juice you can get out of various PDB decompositions
* Provided at the time the state of the art heuristic strength
## Key Insights
* PDBs don't have to have completely disjoint patterns to be combinable
* What we actually can do is think about how the patterns interact and how the actions in the abstract space interact.
